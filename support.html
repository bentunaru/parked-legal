How Parked works

  • Tap Park Here to save your current spot. Parked shows the street name and the time.
  • When you come back, the card shows the distance and a compass arrow pointing to your car.
  • Tap Walk There for walking directions in Apple Maps.
  • Tap Found It to clear the spot.
  • On Apple Watch, park and find from your wrist. Parking on one device updates the other.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Parked has no account and no sign-in. Your parking spot stays on your devices.

Why does Parked ask for my location?

Location is used only while the app is open, to save your parking spot and to show the distance and direction back to it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Parked.

The compass arrow doesn’t appear

The arrow needs a device with a compass and a heading signal. Indoors, in underground car parks, or on older Apple Watch models the arrow may be hidden; the distance and the map still work. Moving a few steps in the open usually brings it back.

The distance seems wrong right after parking

GPS accuracy improves over a few seconds. If Parked shows “Improving accuracy…”, wait a moment before tapping Park Here, or tap Park Here Instead in Settings to replace the spot.

Walk There opens the browser instead of Apple Maps

Parked hands off to the Apple Maps app. If Maps has been removed from your iPhone, reinstall it from the App Store; until then, Parked opens the same directions in Safari.

My Apple Watch doesn’t show the spot I saved on my iPhone

Open Parked on both devices once and keep them nearby. The spot is sent directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch; it never goes through a server. If the watch was out of range, it catches up the next time the app opens.

Metric or imperial distances?

Parked follows your region by default. Choose Metric or Imperial in the app’s Settings; the Apple Watch app follows the iPhone setting.

How is my privacy handled?

See our Privacy Policy. In short: no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and your parking spot never leaves your devices.
