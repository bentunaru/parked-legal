Parked

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version. Parked remembers where you parked and guides you back. There is no account and no login. We do not track you, we show no ads, we run no third-party analytics, and we never sell your data. Your parking spot lives on your iPhone and, if you use it, on your Apple Watch. Nothing is sent to us.

Who we are

Parked (“the app”, “we”) is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that saves your parking spot and shows the way back to your car. For any privacy question, contact benjamin.tunaru@gmail.com.

Location

Parked uses your device’s location only while the app is open, to save your parking spot and to show the distance and direction back to it. The app never uses background location and never records your movements. Your saved spot (coordinates, time, an optional note and a street name) is stored only on your device. You can revoke location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Parked.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, your parking spot is exchanged directly between your own iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple’s device-to-device connection (WatchConnectivity). It does not pass through our servers or anyone else’s.

Street names (Apple Maps)

To show a street name for a parking spot, the app asks Apple Maps to convert coordinates into an address. That request goes to Apple and is handled under Apple’s privacy policy. No account, name, email or device identifier is attached by the app.

Sharing and directions

If you choose Share or Walk There, Parked hands your parking spot to the app you pick, such as Messages or Apple Maps. Those apps handle the data under their own policies. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no logins, no passwords.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No servers of our own: the app has nothing to send your data to.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your devices, you are in control. Tap Found It or Forget Parking Spot in Settings to delete the saved spot. Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device. We do not hold any copy of your personal data.

Children

Parked is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email benjamin.tunaru@gmail.com.
